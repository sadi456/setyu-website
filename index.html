What we build

Everything your business runs on, connected.

Choose the parts you need and we build them into one bespoke system, shaped around your workflow. Every part links to the others, so a booking, an inspection or a support request always ties back to the right client and project.

Management system

Projects, clients, stock and staff in one connected record, with a live dashboard of the numbers that matter.

Booking

Shared diaries for staff, rooms and equipment, with automatic confirmations and reminders. No more double-booking.

Auditing

Digital checklists for inspections and compliance, with photos, notes and a full history ready for inspectors.

Ticketing

Customer emails become tickets your team can assign, prioritise and close, so nothing slips through the cracks.

AI agents

AI assistants built into the business management system we create for you. They answer questions from your own data, draft replies and handle routine tasks, with your team approving anything important.

Add-ons

Invoicing, rotas, CRM, reporting and connections to tools like Xero and Microsoft 365, added whenever you need them.

About us

Setyu is a UK-based company building custom business management systems for small and medium-sized businesses around the world.

Too many businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, inboxes and apps that don't talk to each other. Tasks get missed, information is entered twice and hours are lost every week.

We replace that patchwork with a single system made for you. Every Setyu system is built on our proven foundation, so you get software tailored to your business without the cost and wait of starting from scratch.

Security and your data

Your data belongs to you.

Your business information is the most important thing you trust us with. Here is how we look after it.

You own your data

We never own, sell or share your data. It stays yours, and you can export all of it at any time.

Kept separate

Each client's data is kept apart from every other client's, so it is never mixed or visible to anyone else.

Encrypted

Your data is encrypted while it travels over the internet and while it is stored.

Backed up daily

Automatic daily backups are stored away from your main system, so your information can be restored if something goes wrong.

Access by role

Everyone on your team sees only what their role needs, and access can be removed instantly.

UK GDPR

We handle your data in line with UK GDPR and sign a data processing agreement with every client.
